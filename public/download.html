Your work, wherever you are

One notebook. Multiple ways to work.

Browser first, local always.

Use QUANTA Web without installing anything. Browser notebooks persist locally using IndexedDB, and the app supports offline caching.

Google Drive, optional.

Connect your Google account from settings. Files sync while the app is open and authorized, with retry for offline edits.

Math without compromise.

Write formatted notes and equations or switch to a full LaTeX workspace. Export your work in common formats.

Google Drive requires an OAuth Web client to be configured in QUANTA settings. Browser authorization may need renewal. Browser storage can be cleared by the user or browser; keep exports or enable Drive sync for important work.
